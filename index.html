About Us

A hair studio built on craft and care

Tucked into Manukau Road in Epsom, The Hair Lab (La Tonique) is a full-service hairdresser known for precision cutting, considered colour work and a warm, unhurried studio experience. Our stylists take the time to understand your hair, your lifestyle and what actually works between appointments — not just what looks good in the chair.
